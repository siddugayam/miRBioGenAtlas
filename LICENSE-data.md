## Underlying data attribution

Primary multi-omic, clinical, structural, and population-genetic streams harmonised in
`miRBioGenAtlas` originate from open-access public consortia, including the **TCGA
Research Network / NCI Genomic Data Commons** (https://gdc.cancer.gov), **cBioPortal**
(https://www.cbioportal.org; including MSK-IMPACT `msk_impact_2017`), **CPTAC**
(https://proteomic.datacommons.cancer.gov), **Broad Institute DepMap**
(https://depmap.org; CC BY 4.0), **GTEx v10** (https://gtexportal.org), **Human Protein
Atlas** (https://www.proteinatlas.org; CC BY-SA / open access), **gnomAD v4**
(https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org), **NCBI ClinVar & dbSNP**
(https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/), **RCSB PDB / AlphaFold Protein Structure
Database** (https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk; CC BY 4.0), **AlphaMissense** (CC BY 4.0), and
**Open Targets Platform** (https://platform.opentargets.org). Users must comply with the
[NCI Genomic Data Commons data-use guidance](https://gdc.cancer.gov/about-data/data-analysis-policies)
and cite the primary consortium publications as appropriate.
