Usage guidance:
- In all figures, these seven cohorts are marked with an asterisk (*).
- In `tcga_pancan_atlas_sample_set_summary.csv` and
  `miRNA_biogenesis_top_tumours_excluding_TP53.csv`, they carry
  `low_coverage_flag_N_lt_100 = 1` / `low_coverage_N_lt_100 = 1`.
- Rare somatic point-mutation frequencies (<2%) have wide binomial confidence
  intervals in cohorts with N < 100; supplementation with targeted sequencing
  series (e.g. COSMIC or AACR Project GENIE) is recommended for rare-variant
  inference in these tumour types.
